What to expect

How the sessions work

Short presentations

Each accepted contribution gets a 10-minute slot in front of the whole room — enough to state the problem, the method and the finding, and to send people to the right poster.

Poster sessions

The heart of the conference. Long, unhurried sessions where the detail of the work can actually be discussed. All participants produce a poster.

Demos

Bring the tool, the model or the interface and run it. Monday morning is given over to two demo sessions.
