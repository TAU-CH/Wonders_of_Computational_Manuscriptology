Getting there

Travel and stay

Arriving

Ben Gurion International Airport (TLV) is about 20 km from the university, roughly 25–40 minutes by train or taxi. Tel Aviv University has its own railway station on the main line.

Staying

A list of suggested hotels near the campus, and any negotiated rates, will be published here closer to the date.
