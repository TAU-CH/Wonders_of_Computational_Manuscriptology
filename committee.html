Context

The MiDRASH project

MiDRASH is a Synergy Grant project funded by the European Research Council, bringing together computer scientists, paleographers and philologists around the medieval Hebrew manuscript record. Its principal investigators are based at partner institutions in France and Israel.

This conference marks the midpoint of the project: an occasion to put the results of the first half in front of the wider community, and to be honest about the problems that remain.
